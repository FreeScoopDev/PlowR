### Added
- Barlow, the font chosen for the 1.7 design's headings, numbers and
  buttons, is bundled with the app (Medium, SemiBold, Bold, ExtraBold;
  free under the SIL Open Font License, whose text is in Settings → Legal →

  for reading at a glance early in the morning or late at night. Screens
  start using it as they move to the new design.
