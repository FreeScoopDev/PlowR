### Added
- A Sales Tax Rate in Business Profile, put on every new invoice and
  proposal: ones built by hand, and the ones PlowR makes from recorded
  services, Bill Unbilled Work and contract payments, which were always 0%
  before. Any document can still change its own rate; revisions keep their
  original's. Many states tax snow removal or landscaping, and an invoice
  that leaves tax out costs the business its margin. The setting says to
  check the state's rules; it isn't tax advice.

### Fixed
- A tax rate typed with a decimal comma ("6,625", what the keypad types in
  many regions) was saved as 0% on every screen that takes a rate.
