### Added
- **Expenses and mileage** (Home → Expenses). Log what the business spends (Fuel, Materials, Equipment & Repairs, Other), what was bought, and optionally for which client or route, and a mileage log of business trips (distance as typed, its purpose). Both are listed by month with each month's total; tap to change, swipe to delete (asked first, naming the item and amount). At tax time an operator needed fuel, materials and mileage in one place, and PlowR's money picture was income only. "What" suggests only the business's own earlier items, so the screens stay the same for snow, lawn and landscaping work. Free and Pro both log; a read-only plan sees, deletes and exports. PlowR doesn't measure distance: it keeps no odometer and its route distances are straight lines.
- **Export Data** has two more files, Expenses and Mileage, oldest first, with the count and total on each. Receipt photos (coming in a later change) aren't in the files.
- **Business Profile → Season Starts**: the month a business's season begins, for season totals of expenses (used by the coming expenses summary).

### Internal
- `Expense` and `MileageEntry` are new CloudKit record types (all fields, including `receiptData` as an asset and `receiptCapturedAt`), and `BusinessProfile` has a new field, `seasonStartMonth`: deploy the schema to Production before the next release. Every field for the receipts and summary changes is here, so the schema is deployed once. Neither model has relationships; a client or route is referred to by ID with a copy of its name, so an expense outlives a deleted client or route and `ClientRemoval` doesn't touch it.
