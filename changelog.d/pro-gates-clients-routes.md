### Added
- PlowR Pro's free tier takes effect: up to 10 current clients and one
  route (the first one made). Every way to add a client (Add Manually,
  Scan with Camera, Add Visit's and documents' New Client, Add to PlowR
  leads) or bring one back (Mark Active, Reopen, scheduling an inactive
  client), make a route (New Route, Duplicate, Create Route from the
  Schedule) or start one (Start, Start From Here) checks the plan, and when
  it needs Pro a sheet says why and opens the subscribe screen. Import
  Clients is a Pro tool. Other routes show "Pro" on a free plan. A route
  already running is never stopped.
- After a subscription ends, the Dashboard says so: read only with more
  than 10 clients (records safe, export and payments open), or the free
  tier with 10 or fewer.
