### Added
- PlowR Pro's free tier takes effect: up to 10 active clients (leads
  included; a client marked inactive frees a place) and one route (the
  first one made). Every way to add a client (Add Manually, Scan with
  Camera, Add Visit's and documents' New Client, Add to PlowR leads) or
  bring one back (Mark Active, scheduling an inactive client), make a route
  (New Route, Duplicate, Create Route from the Schedule) or start one
  (Start, Start From Here) checks the plan, and when it needs Pro a sheet
  says why and opens the subscribe screen. Import Clients is a Pro tool.
  On a free plan the route list marks the free route "Free" and the others
  "Pro". A route already running is never stopped.
- After a subscription ends, the Dashboard says so: read only with more
  than 10 clients (records safe, export and payments open), or the free
  tier with 10 or fewer.
