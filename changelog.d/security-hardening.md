### Security
- The Today's Route widget hides the next client's name and address while
  the phone is locked (StandBy, an iPad's Lock Screen). The Live Activity
  still shows them: reading it on a locked phone while driving is its job.
- Invoices, reports and spreadsheets shared last time are removed from the
  phone when PlowR next opens, and Delete Account also clears saved weather
  and elevation answers. They held client names, addresses and amounts.
- A client's email opens Mail only when it is an address: one imported as
  "a@b.com?bcc=…" opened Mail with someone else copied in.
- Import Clients refuses a file whose size it can't tell, and says how to
  choose it instead.
