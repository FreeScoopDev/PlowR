### Added
- After a PlowR Pro subscription ends with more than 10 active clients
  (read only), records can be seen, exported and paid, but not made or
  changed until the business subscribes again (Joe's rule). Every editor
  (visits, logged work, invoices and proposals, billing, contracts,
  properties and service areas, pins, stops, routes, the business profile,
  payment methods, the service catalog) says why and offers Subscribe Again
  instead of its form, wherever it's opened from. Actions that change a
  record in place (Convert, Revise, Duplicate, a contract's
  Renew, Mark Signed, Book Visits, Cancel and Make Invoice, the Schedule's
  Complete and Skip, Make Invoices, Optimize Order, Lost and Reopen, Mark
  Responded, adding photos) do the same. The client page, a job's page and
  a property still show everything, with their fields locked; the client
  page offers Mark Inactive (the way back to the free tier) and a property
  keeps Remove Property. Payments, sending an invoice still owed, Export
  Data, deleting records and a route already running stay open. Nothing
  changes on the free tier or Pro.
