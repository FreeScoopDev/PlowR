### Added
- Tap a visit on Schedule or on Home's Today to open it: its client, place, time, status and expected services, with big buttons for **Record Services**, **Complete** and **Complete & Invoice**. A call-out or a one-off mow that isn't run as a route had no way to add photos, change the services or bill on the spot; Schedule's swipe Complete recorded the expected services as they were. Record Services is the same sheet as on a route stop, with the visit's expected services already ticked at its prices (after hours included), so saving records exactly what Complete would unless you change it. Complete & Invoice saves, completes and opens the draft invoice to check and send. A completed visit offers **Edit Services**, **Invoice Work** while its work is unbilled, or **View Invoice**. Everything writes the visit's one Service Log record, the one a route stop for the same visit shares, so nothing is recorded or billed twice. The sheet says why an action isn't there (no client on file, a skipped visit, a contract covering the work, a comped client). Swipes stay as shortcuts; Edit Visit and Open Client are on the sheet.
