### Changed
- **"Awaiting Response" on a client's page now follows invoices and proposals sent to them.** Marking an invoice sent, or sharing any invoice or proposal to someone from the share sheet (Messages, Mail, AirDrop and so on; not copying, printing or saving it), shows the client as awaiting a response until you tap Mark Responded. It used to be set by a text sent from the client's page, while sending a document didn't set it. Texts don't set it now, and marks left by earlier texts are cleared once, so none reads as a document sent.
