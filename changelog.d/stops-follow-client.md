### Fixed
- **Route stops follow their client.** A client's name, phone, address and map pin were copied onto each route stop when it was made and never again, so a corrected phone number or address, or a pin set by hand, never reached the routes the client was already on: the stop kept texting the old number and the map, arrival times and job-site alerts used the old house. Now every change to the client updates their stops on all routes, and stops that had already fallen behind catch up the next time PlowR opens. A stop's own notes and what a run recorded are left as they were.
