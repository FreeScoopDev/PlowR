### Fixed
- **Route stops follow their client.** A client's name, phone, address and map pin were copied onto each route stop when it was made and never again, so a corrected phone number or address, or a pin set by hand, never reached the routes the client was already on: the stop kept texting the old number, and the map, drive times and job-site alerts used the old house. Now a change on the client's page, with Adjust Pin or with the property scanner's Move Pin updates their stops on every route, and so does a change that iCloud brings from another device. On a route in progress, the current stop's job-site alert moves with its pin. Stops that had already fallen behind catch up when PlowR opens or comes back to the front. A stop's own notes and what a run recorded are left as they were.
- **Edit Client keeps a pin set with Adjust Pin or Move Pin.** Tapping Save afterwards wrote back the address the screen had opened with, and an address typed before could be looked up again over the pin just set by hand.
