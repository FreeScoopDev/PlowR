### Internal
- The Release build has no Swift 6 concurrency warnings left (there were 19 in 10 files). Each was a pure helper or value type, isolated to the main actor by the project's default, called from a context that isn't: SwiftUI view initializers (contract and property drafts, the business colour), CSV rows, place lookups, contract pricing, the weather service's decoding and coordinate rounding, the calendar's title, and the Live Activity's attributes. They're now marked `nonisolated` (or called through a closure that keeps the caller's isolation). Nothing they do needs the main actor: they read values and build values. Swift 6 makes these errors, so clearing them now keeps that move small, and a new warning stands out.
