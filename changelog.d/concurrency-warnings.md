### Internal
- The Release build has no Swift 6 concurrency warnings left (there were 19 in 10 files). Two causes: a function passed by name (`map(Contracts.Draft.init)`, `map(render)`, `map(of)`, `flatMap(Color.init(hex:))`) is checked as having no actor, so those are closures now; and code with no actor called main-actor helpers: `StopRecording` (contract pricing), contract drafts (place lookups, contract names), the calendar store (its title), the `WeatherService` actor (coordinate rounding, decoding, weather codes) and ActivityKit (the Live Activity's attributes). Those helpers, which only read and build values, are marked `nonisolated`. Nothing moves to another thread. Swift 6 makes these errors, so clearing them now keeps that move small, and a new warning stands out.
