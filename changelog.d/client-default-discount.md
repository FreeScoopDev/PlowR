### Fixed
- A client's Default Discount now comes off their new invoices and proposals. The client page let you set 5–50%, but nothing priced with it, so a client set to 10% with $400 of work was billed $400. It is now the starting discount in the proposal and invoice builder (it follows the lines as you add them; type an amount, or 0, to change it) and on invoices PlowR makes for you: from Record Services and from Bill Unbilled Work. A contract's own price isn't discounted again, because it's the price the contract agreed: not its payments, and not a per-visit contract's visit charge. Extras billed alongside are discounted. Invoices already made are unchanged.
