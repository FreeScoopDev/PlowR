### Fixed
- A price, discount or tax rate PlowR can't read is now pointed out, not quietly replaced. In a US region, "1,250" typed with a hardware keyboard or pasted (the comma read as a thousands separator) billed the catalog price instead. The same text as a discount became $0, and as a custom item the line was dropped. A tax of "105" or "8..25" became 0%. A negative line amount was accepted. Now the proposal and invoice builder and Record Services name the field they can't read, say what to type, and won't save until it's fixed. On a document's Edit screen, a discount or tax rate it can't read isn't saved, so the document keeps its old one, and the screen says so. A line amount there can't go below $0.
