### Changed
- **The standard services are offered, not added by themselves.** Settings → Service Catalog, and a route stop's services when the catalog is empty, offer Snow Removal, Lawn Care and Cleanup: one tap adds that category's common services with typical prices. PlowR used to add all eleven the first time the catalog opened, and again whenever one was missing. So a service the user deleted came back, a business that went straight to a route had no services to record and had to leave the route for Settings, and a second iPhone or iPad added its own set before the first device's arrived through iCloud, so every standard service showed twice. A lawn business also got snow services it never asked for. A category is offered while the business has no services in it; asking again adds only the ones missing.
