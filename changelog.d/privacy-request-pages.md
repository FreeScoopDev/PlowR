### Changed
- The privacy policy covers the pages on getplowr.app that a request link and Add to PlowR open: a prospect's request is built on their device and goes to the business only in a text or email they send themselves; nothing they type reaches the website, since everything is after the "#"; a page opened inside another app (Facebook, Instagram and the like) is in that app's browser, under its policy; the page reads the browser's user agent on the device only to choose the text link and warn about in-app browsers; GitHub Pages hosts the site and logs visitors' IP addresses like any host; and a request is saved in the business's PlowR only when the business taps Save Lead, when its address goes to Apple Maps for the pin.
