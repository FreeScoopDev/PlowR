### Added
- PlowR Pro's tools need Pro: the Service Report, the storm card's actions
  (texting clients, marking below trigger; the forecast itself shows for
  everyone), Below Trigger on the route screen, a contract's snow trigger
  setting and marking days below it, and the Request Link (Settings and
  the Pipeline). Without Pro each says why and opens the subscribe screen;
  existing values stay visible.
- Free-tier invoices, proposals, contracts and the Season Report carry
  "Prepared with PlowR · getplowr.app" (Joe: every document sent puts the
  name out there); PlowR Pro's carry only the business's name. The Service
  Report no longer names PlowR.
- After a subscription ends with more than 10 clients, documents can still
  be opened and read, but only an invoice with money owed can be shared
  (that's how the business gets paid); proposals, contracts, paid invoices
  and the Season Report wait for a subscription.
