### Added
- Storm trigger. When the forecast for your area has snow today or in the next two days at or above a client's contract trigger ("2 in or more"), the Dashboard shows a storm card: the day, about how much, which contract clients' triggers it reaches and which it doesn't, and your routes, each opening its page to Message All Clients and Start. Without any contract trigger, a business offering a snow service sees it from 1 in. The evening weather alert says the same the night before. It's the weather model's estimate for the whole day over your area, not a measurement at each property, and the card says so; PlowR never starts a route or sends a text by itself.
