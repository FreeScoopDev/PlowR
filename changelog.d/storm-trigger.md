### Added
- Storm trigger. When the forecast for your area has snow today or in the next two days that reaches a client's contract trigger ("2 in or more", or "Every snowfall"), the Dashboard shows a storm card: the day, about how much, which contract clients' triggers it reaches and which it doesn't, a button to text the clients it reaches (with storm quick texts), and your routes, each opening its page to Message All Clients and Start. "Every snowfall" counts from a tenth of an inch. Without any contract trigger, a business offering a snow service sees the card from 1 in; a business without snow services never does. Contracts of deleted or inactive clients don't count. The evening weather alert speaks for the storm the night before, as of when PlowR last checked, unless an earlier day has its own warning (freezing rain tomorrow keeps tonight's alert). It's the weather model's estimate for the whole day over your area, not a measurement at each property, and the card says so; PlowR never starts a route or sends a text by itself.

### Changed
- A contract's snow trigger footer says what it's now used for.
