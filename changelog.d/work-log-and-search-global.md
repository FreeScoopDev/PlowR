### Added
- **Search on Home**: the magnifying glass next to Help searches clients, documents, routes and Service Log jobs at once, with results grouped by kind (five of each, then **Show All**). It finds them the way the Clients and Documents lists do (every word, capitals and accents aside): a client's name, address, phone or tag; a document's client, invoice number or notes; a route's name; a job's client, address or notes. Tapping a result opens the screen that already shows it. Until now finding "the job where we noted the broken gate" meant knowing which tab it was in and scrolling. No CloudKit schema change.
