### Internal
- A CSV reader for Import Clients (`CSVReader`), with no third-party code: UTF-8 with or without a byte-order mark, UTF-16 with one, and otherwise Windows Latin-1 (what Excel on Windows writes); comma, semicolon or tab, whichever the header uses; quoted fields with separators, line breaks and doubled quotes; any line ending; blank rows dropped and short rows padded; and the apostrophe CSVExport and spreadsheets put before formula-like text taken off. An .xlsx or .numbers file is recognized and refused, rather than read as garbage. PlowR's own export reads back as it went out.
