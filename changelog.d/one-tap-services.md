### Added
- On the route screen, a stop that has expected services, with nothing recorded for it yet, gets a main button that names them ("Done: Mow, Trim"). One tap completes the stop and records those services at your catalog prices, exactly as Record Services' Add Expected and Save would. Before, Complete recorded the visit and its time but no services, so a crew that just tapped Complete all day ended up with records saying "No services" and nothing for Bill Unbilled Work to bill. The button always says what it records: a longer list shows the first names and "+N more", and VoiceOver reads them all. Complete Without Services, under it, completes the stop as before. Cancelling the text to the next client records nothing, and Undo takes the services back off with the stop. Siri, Control Center and Below Trigger still record no services, since they don't show which ones would be recorded. Services already recorded, typed prices and work already on an invoice are never changed.
