### Changed
- The privacy policy is rewritten from a line-by-line check against the app, for the App Store. It now covers what had been added since it was written (contracts, payments, properties, each job's record, the business profile and payment details), and says plainly what leaves the device: addresses and your position to Apple Maps for maps, directions and arrival estimates; rounded coordinates to the weather service and a measured property's corners to the elevation service; the stop handed to Google Maps or Waze if you choose them; and that, like any internet request, these carry your IP address. It says that during a route PlowR follows your location in the background and watches a finished stop for up to two hours, what the Lock Screen, widget and Siri show, what calendar events contain, what the client side keeps and how Delete My Data removes it, and what uninstalling leaves behind.
- The policy describes Delete Account & Data's two choices (Remove from This Device, Delete Everything), the per-device sync switch, and that a picked photo's location is removed before it's saved.
- Delete Account & Data's Delete Everything reaches iCloud and your other devices, and Remove from This Device leaves them alone: both checked on a device with a control before the policy said so.
