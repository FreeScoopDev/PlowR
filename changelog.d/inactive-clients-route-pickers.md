### Fixed
- **Inactive clients are no longer put on new routes.** The 1.1.0 notes said they are hidden from route building, but the New Route and Edit Route pickers listed them with every other client, Schedule's Create Route added them when a visit was still scheduled from before they were marked inactive, and duplicating a route copied them. All four now leave inactive clients out, and Create Route says how many visits it left off. A stop already on a route stays until it's removed.
