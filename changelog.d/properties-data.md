### Internal
- Properties, the data side: a client can have additional properties (`Property`: a label, address, pin, stop notes, goal time, expected services, active). The client's own address is their main property. It isn't a record, and it carries the client's ID wherever a property ID is kept, as the Service Log already did. So nothing moved: no data migration, and a client with one place is exactly as before. `Place` is the one rule that turns a client and a property ID into an address, a pin, expected services, a goal and pricing zones.
- Route stops and scheduled visits keep the property they're at (`propertyID`, empty for the main one). A repeating visit's next one keeps it too. Stops and visits follow their place's address and pin, as they followed the client's. A job in the Service Log is recorded at its stop's or visit's property, and priced for it. A stop completes only the day's visit at its own place. A stop's expected services and target time come from its place, and "all routes" changes that place's usual services. A route made from the Schedule puts each stop where its visit was booked, and a duplicated route keeps its stops' properties. Deleting a client deletes their properties.
- Measured zones are the main property's only for now. Work at an additional property is never priced by the main house's measurements: a per-square-foot service there is priced as for any client who hasn't been measured (its rate, to be typed over in Record Services).
- A property ID that doesn't resolve (the property was removed, or hasn't synced to this device yet) is a missing place, never the main one. Its stops and visits keep the address they have. Its visits are never completed by the main house's stop. Its jobs keep its ID. "All routes" does nothing there, and a new route leaves it off. Without that, a leftover visit at a removed rental could be completed, and billed, by the main house's stop.
- `PropertyRemoval` takes a property's stops with it, and its visits not done yet from the start of today (one left behind would have nowhere to be). It matches the property's client as well as its ID. Its done visits and jobs stay as history. A visit edited over to another client drops the first client's property (`ClientVisits.book`).
- Every device must be on this build before a property is added. A 1.2.0 build doesn't know `propertyID`: it moves a property's stops and visits back to the client's address, and a new build moves them again, on every sync. There are no screens for properties yet: that's the next change. New CloudKit fields to deploy: `CD_Property` (and its `client` relationship), `RouteStop.propertyID`, `ScheduledVisit.propertyID`.
