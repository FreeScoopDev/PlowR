### Added
- **Add to PlowR:** the link at the end of a request from your Request Link opens PlowR at a **New Lead** screen, filled in with what the person sent: name, phone, email, address, the service and how often, and their notes. Check and change anything, then Save Lead: they're added to your Pipeline as a lead, tagged with the day you saved them, and put on the map in the background (retried if there's no signal, or marked "Address not found on the map" if the map doesn't know the address). If the number is already a client's, the screen says so, with their page a tap away, and says so again at Save if their client arrived from iCloud meanwhile. Nothing is saved until you tap Save Lead, since anyone could have sent the text.
- The link works from a closed app: it waits for PlowR's sign-in check, and if you're signed out it opens once you've signed in. It opens on top of whatever is on screen, in a layer of its own, so nothing open underneath (the route screen, a half-written proposal) is closed or loses what was typed. A second request opened while one is on screen waits and opens after it. A damaged link, or one opened on a device set up for finding a service, says so instead of doing nothing.

### Internal
- Universal Links: the app claims getplowr.app/lead/ (Associated Domains, and `docs/.well-known/apple-app-site-association`, which `docs/_config.yml` now includes). Where the link opens in a browser instead, the page's Open in PlowR button uses plowr://lead. **The App ID needs the Associated Domains capability before the next Release Flow archive.**
