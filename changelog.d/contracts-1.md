### Added
- Contracts: an agreement with a client for a period, on their page under Contracts. Each has its dates, the places it applies to (their own address or other properties), the services it covers, and how it's priced: one season price (up front or in 2 to 12 payments), a price per visit, or a monthly amount. A contract covering a snow service can record its snow trigger ("2 in or more"). A new contract is a draft until it's marked signed (which asks first). Once signed, what was agreed is locked: where, what, the price and its payments. Only its name, notes, trigger and end date (not before today) change; a bigger change is cancelling it and making a new one. A signed contract can be cancelled but not deleted, and it makes the client a customer in the Pipeline. Deleting a client with Keep Records keeps their signed contracts (with the client's name), as it keeps invoices; a draft goes with them. Before, a season's agreement lived only on paper, and each visit was priced from the catalog as if it were a one-off. (Joe chose the recommended answers to all nine questions in the Contracts plan.)
- This is the first step. Next: work covered by a contract stops showing as owed, per-visit contracts set the visit price, and season and monthly contracts make their invoices.
