### Fixed
- **Repeating visits fall on the days chosen.** Add Visit created a series with its own date arithmetic, which ignored the weekdays picked: weekly on Monday and Thursday, starting on a Wednesday, gave 52 Wednesdays. Creating a series and continuing it now use one rule (`RecurrenceRule`), and every date is counted from the series' first visit.
- **Completing a repeating visit no longer duplicates the next one.** The series is created up front, but completing any of its visits added "the next one" again, so every completion left a duplicate, and those copies lost the visit's reason and expected services. A series now gets another visit only when nothing after the completed one is still scheduled, dated after the series' latest visit, and carrying everything the series has. Skipping the last visit no longer ends the series, and a skipped visit is never re-created.
- **Visits keep their time of day and day of month.** A visit that wrapped into the next week was placed at midnight, so it showed as overdue all day. Repeating every two or more weeks skipped the week's other chosen days. A monthly series from the 31st slid to the 28th after February and stayed there. A series with a visit at 2:30 AM moved to 3:00 for good after the spring clock change.
- **The end date includes its whole day, and a series is created at most two years ahead,** so "every 30 months" no longer books 52 visits reaching 130 years out.
