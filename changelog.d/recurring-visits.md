### Fixed
- **Repeating visits fall on the days chosen.** Add Visit created a series with its own date arithmetic, which ignored the weekdays picked: weekly on Monday and Thursday, starting on a Wednesday, gave 52 Wednesdays. Creating a series and continuing it now use one rule (`RecurrenceRule`).
- **Completing a repeating visit no longer duplicates the next one.** The series is created up front, but completing any of its visits added "the next one" again, so every completion left a duplicate, and those copies lost the visit's reason and expected services. A visit is now added only when the series has nothing scheduled after the one completed, and it carries everything the series has. A future visit you deleted stays deleted.
- **A visit that wraps into the next week keeps its time of day.** It was placed at midnight, so it showed as overdue all day. Repeating every two or more weeks also skipped the week's other chosen days. A monthly series starting on the 31st slid to the 28th after February and stayed there.
- **The end date includes its whole day, and a series is created at most two years ahead,** so "every 30 months" no longer books 52 visits reaching 130 years out.
