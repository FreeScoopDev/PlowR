### Fixed
- **Totals agree everywhere, from one formula.** The builder's "Estimated total" left out tax and custom lines, so it was lower than the document it saved. The invoice Edit screen showed a second "Total" with the discount taken off twice. A document's totals now come from one place (`Proposal.totals`), and tax is rounded to the cent once, so the PDF's rows always add up to its total.
- **Each line is added up at the cent it prints as.** A line holding fractions of a cent, like 10.125, printed as 10.13 but was added up unrounded, so the PDF's rows could miss its total by a cent. Amounts are now rounded to the cent as they're typed, and older lines are added up as printed. An older document with such lines can change by up to a cent per line, including a sent invoice that isn't paid yet. Its new total is the one its rows add up to.
- **Tax rates keep three decimals.** Opening an invoice's Edit screen and tapping Done saved 8.875 % as 8.9 %, because the field showed one decimal, and the PDF printed "Tax (8.9%)". A typed rate is now kept to three decimals, and every rate is shown as it's charged, so the Edit screen, the PDF label and the rate charged agree. A rate typed with four decimals, like 7.0625, is saved as 7.063; one saved that way before is shown and charged as it was.
- **The Edit screen keeps what was changed on it, however it's closed.** Line amounts saved as they were typed, but the discount, tax and due date saved only on Done, so swiping the screen away or tapping Mark as Sent kept some edits and dropped others. All three now save them. Only fields changed on the screen are saved, so opening an older invoice and closing it leaves its amounts exactly as they were, and a change synced from another device meanwhile isn't overwritten. An invoice without a due date gets the one the screen shows.
- **A cleared amount in the builder uses the after-hours price shown beside it,** not the plain catalog price. The price shown, the estimate and the saved document now come from one place (`DocumentDraft`).
- **Payment reminders texted to clients give the amount to the cent.** A $149.50 invoice was texted as $150.
- **The PDF's discount row shows the discount actually taken,** never more than the work it comes off.
