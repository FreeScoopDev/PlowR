### Fixed
- **PlowR says when your data isn't backed up to iCloud.** A card at the top of the Dashboard, and the Data & Backup row in Settings, say so when PlowR can't use iCloud on this device (not signed in, or iCloud off for PlowR), when iCloud is restricted, when the Apple Account needs attention, and when PlowR couldn't open its iCloud database at launch. In the first three cases clients, routes and documents are only on this device. PlowR used to warn only in that last, rare case: someone not signed in to iCloud was never told, and Settings kept saying "Data Backed Up to iCloud". Settings now says that only when iCloud has said so. The card can be closed; it comes back for a different problem, or for the same one after iCloud has worked in between. It replaces an alert that came back every time the Dashboard opened. When iCloud can't answer, the Dashboard shows nothing: there's nothing for the user to fix.
