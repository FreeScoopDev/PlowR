### Fixed
- **PlowR says when your data isn't backed up to iCloud.** A card at the top of the Dashboard, and the Data & Backup row in Settings, say so when this iPhone isn't signed in to iCloud, when iCloud is restricted or the Apple Account needs attention, or when PlowR couldn't open its iCloud database. In each case clients, routes and documents are only on this iPhone. PlowR used to warn only in that last, rare case: someone not signed in to iCloud was never told, and Settings kept saying "Data Backed Up to iCloud". The card can be closed and comes back only if the situation changes; it replaces an alert that came back every time the Dashboard opened. When iCloud can't be reached (no signal on a job site), nothing is shown: there's nothing to fix.
