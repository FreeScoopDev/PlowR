### Internal
- **No force-unwrapped `URL(string:)` left in the app.** SwiftLint's `force_unwrapping` rule doesn't flag a `!` after an initializer call, so six slipped past the lint gate: the Google Maps and Waze checks on the route screen, and the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service links in Settings. All six were fixed strings and never failed, but a typo in one would have crashed the app. The route screen now asks a `canOpen(_:)` helper that answers no for a URL it can't build, and Settings shows a link only if its URL builds. Nothing changes on screen. `CLAUDE.md` gives the grep that finds any new one, since the lint won't.
