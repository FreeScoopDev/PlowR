### Added
- The request page on getplowr.app that a business's Request Service link opens (`docs/r/`). A prospect sees the business's name, a welcome line and the services it offers, fills in a short form (name, phone, address, service, how often, notes, optional email) and taps Send Request. Their own Messages opens, addressed to the business, with a tidy request ending in an **Add to PlowR** link, and nothing is sent until they tap send. On a computer it offers email (if the business gave one) or Copy Request. Inside Facebook or Instagram, which can block texting links, it says to open the page in Safari or Chrome. Nothing typed is sent to or kept by the website: it's all built in the browser, from the part of the link after the `#`.
- The Add to PlowR page (`docs/lead/`), for when the link opens in a browser instead of the app: it shows the request and an Open in PlowR button.
