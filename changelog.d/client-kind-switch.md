### Added
- A client's page can switch them between lead and customer. A lead or quote has **Mark as Customer** beside Mark Lost, and a client who is a customer only because they were added or imported as one has **Mark as Lead**. Before, picking the wrong one on New Client could not be undone: someone you meant to quote never showed in the Pipeline or got a follow-up reminder, and a regular stayed a lead until work was booked. A customer with a visit, a route stop, an invoice or a contract in force has no switch, since they are a customer by what happened.

### Fixed
- The Contact menu on a client's page no longer offers Call or Message for a phone entry with no digits, such as "n/a", where tapping them did nothing.
- The client page's Contact tile says "No phone, email or address" when there is no way to reach a client, instead of opening an empty menu.
