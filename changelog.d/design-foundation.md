### Changed
- PlowR's accent is the forest green of the new design everywhere: the
  tab bar, buttons, links and switches. It was navy, and some screens fell
  back to the system's blue because the app's accent colour was never set.
  Invoices and other documents keep your business's own colour.
- Every status has one colour on every screen: money owed amber, overdue
  red, paid or done green, scheduled blue, leads purple, void grey, and
  drafts plain. The same status was orange on one screen and blue on
  another, and a cancelled visit was red like an overdue invoice. Count
  tiles with nothing to flag (stops, active clients, drafts) are plain, so
  colour only shows where it means something.
- Screens' main buttons (Start Route, End Route, Send Message, Create
  Invoices) are the copper action button from the route screen, and the
  tab bar's icons match the rest of the app.
