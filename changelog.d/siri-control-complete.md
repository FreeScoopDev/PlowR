### Fixed
- **Siri's "Complete current stop in PlowR" completes the stop you're at**, without opening the app and whether or not the route screen is showing, and says what happened: which stop is next, that it was the last one, or that no route is in progress. It used to reach the stop only through the route screen while it was on screen, opened the notify prompt instead of completing, did nothing on the last stop, and said "Stop marked complete." either way. App Review checks that advertised features work (guideline 2.1).
- **Control Center's Complete Stop completes the stop its widget shows.** It opens PlowR, which completes that stop if it's still the current one and shows the next; otherwise the route screen says why. It used to only open the app, while its description promised more.
- Neither completes a stop you haven't seen. If iCloud removed the stop you were at, while PlowR was closed or in the background, the route moves to the next one, and Siri and Control Center wait until the route screen has shown it, even if iOS closed PlowR in between. Completing it straight away would have credited that client with a visit that didn't happen. A completion is also saved at once, not left to autosave, which a phone can suspend before it runs.
- The route screen's service recorder stays on the stop it was opened for. If the stop was completed while the recorder was open, saving used to put this client's services, photos and invoice on the next stop. The notify sheet likewise no longer says "changed on another device" when its stop was completed by Siri. A stop's job-site geofence now follows the route, whoever completes the stop.
