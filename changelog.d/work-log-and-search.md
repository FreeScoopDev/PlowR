### Added
- **Work Log** on Home's Quick Actions, after Season Report: every job in the Service Log for every client, grouped by day, with the number of jobs, the time and the value at the prices recorded (before discounts, tax and contracts). The filter button chooses the period (Today, Yesterday, This Week, Last Week, This Month or Custom Dates), one service, and Not Billed or Invoiced work; the period is remembered. Tap a job to open and correct it. Until now the work done could only be seen one client at a time, so "what did we do yesterday, and what's still unbilled?" meant opening every client. No CloudKit schema change.
