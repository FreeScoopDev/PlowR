### Fixed
- The Season Report covers the period you choose from its Share menu
  (This Year, Last 12 Months, Last Year or All Time), and says which on the
  report. It always covered everything since the business started,
  whatever the season. Visits and time on site come from the Service Log for
  that period, and money collected from the payments that came in during it.
  "Owed now" is what's owed today. Money is printed to the cent ($1,234.50)
  instead of rounded to whole dollars, so the report agrees with the
  invoices.
