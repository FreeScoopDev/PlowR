### Changed
- The route screen follows your location only while PlowR is on screen, a
  fix every 20 metres or so, instead of continuously at full accuracy all
  route long, in the background too. Arrival and departure times still come
  from the job-site areas iOS watches by itself, with the app closed, so
  nothing is missed; the map, arrival estimates and weather catch up the
  moment you come back. It saves a crew's battery over a long day, and PlowR
  no longer declares the background location mode, which App Review asks
  every app to justify. The Privacy Policy says so.
