### Added
- Delete Account & Data offers two choices. **Remove from This Device** takes PlowR's data off this device only: your iCloud and your other devices keep it. It's for handing a phone on or retiring a crew device. It signs out, erases what's only on this device (settings, the route in progress, the widget, notifications, shared files), turns iCloud sync off on this device, and removes PlowR's database the next time PlowR opens; PlowR asks you to close it to finish. Anything the device hasn't sent to iCloud yet is lost, and the dialog says so. **Delete Everything** is as before: everything, from this device and from iCloud, so from your other devices too.
- With sync off, Settings → Data & Backup says so and offers **Turn iCloud Sync Back On**: the next time PlowR opens, your iCloud data comes back, and what was added on the device while it was off goes to iCloud. While it's off, Delete Everything isn't offered, since it couldn't reach iCloud; the dialog says how to do it. The iCloud card says sync is off instead of warning.

### Internal
- Why it works this way: iCloud sync follows the device's iCloud account, not PlowR's sign-in, so a copy wiped from a syncing device comes straight back; and deleting records through the database is what tells iCloud to delete them everywhere (seen on a device, to be confirmed). So Remove from This Device deletes no records: it turns sync off (`DeviceSync`) and deletes the database's files at the next launch, before it opens, log first and database last (`PlowRApp.makeContainer`), which then opens with `cloudKitDatabase: .none`, said explicitly: SwiftData's default can still sync. Sync can only be turned off this way, so a device with sync off never holds iCloud's records and turning it back on can only add.
