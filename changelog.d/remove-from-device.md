### Added
- Delete Account & Data offers two choices. **Remove from This Device** takes PlowR's data off this device only: your iCloud and your other devices keep it. It's for handing a phone on or retiring a crew device. It signs out, erases what's only on this device (settings, the route in progress, the widget, notifications, shared files), turns iCloud sync off on this device, and removes PlowR's database the next time PlowR opens. PlowR asks you to close it to finish. **Delete Everything** is as before: everything, from this device and from iCloud, so from your other devices too. When sync is off on this device, it says that it then reaches only this device.
- **Sync with iCloud on This Device** (Settings → Data & Backup): turned off by Remove from This Device, and on again here. Turning it on brings your iCloud data back the next time PlowR opens. With sync off, the iCloud card says so instead of warning.

### Internal
- Why it works this way: iCloud sync follows the device's iCloud account, not PlowR's sign-in, so a copy wiped from a syncing device comes straight back; and deleting records through the database is what tells iCloud to delete them everywhere (checked on a device). So Remove from This Device deletes no records: it turns sync off (`DeviceSync`) and deletes the database's files at the next launch, before it opens (`PlowRApp.makeContainer`), which then opens without iCloud.
