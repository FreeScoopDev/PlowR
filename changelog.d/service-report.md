### Added
- Service Report (a client's Service History). For one of their places, active, inactive or since removed, and a period (a contract's season so far, the last 30 days, this year, or chosen dates), a PDF in the business's colours and logo listing every visit: the day, where its record came from (tracked on a route, completed from the Schedule, or logged by hand), the services, the route, notes, the address the work was recorded at when it has since changed, and its before and after photos. It's what a contractor hands an insurer or a lawyer after a slip-and-fall claim, or a client who disputes a bill; PlowR had all of it, scattered.
- It says only what PlowR recorded, for what it is, because a report for a claim is worthless once one line is shown to overstate. Times are printed only for route stops, the ones PlowR timed: started is when the stop became the current one, so it includes travel there, and completed is when it was marked done. A visit completed from the Schedule or logged by hand says its time wasn't recorded (the Schedule would otherwise have shown the time it was booked for). Photos carry no time, because PlowR stamps them when they're saved, not taken. A note at the end says all this, that records can be edited in PlowR, and the time zone. The same job recorded on two devices before iCloud merged them is listed once. Photos are shrunk as they're drawn, so a season's report stays small enough to email.
- It isn't called "Proof of Service": that's the name of a court form for serving legal papers, and a lawyer would read it that way.

### Internal
- The contract PDF and the Service Report share one page writer (`PDFPageWriter`): the business header and logo, text that runs on to the next page, and rows of photos, shrunk at true size (scale 1, not the screen's 3x).
