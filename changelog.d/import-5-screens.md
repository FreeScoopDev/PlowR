### Added
- **Import Clients** (Settings, the Clients + menu, and the empty Clients screen): bring a client list in from a spreadsheet saved as CSV, from Numbers, Excel, Google Sheets, Google Contacts or another app's export, instead of typing every client in. PlowR guesses what each column holds and shows an example from it, so a wrong guess is easy to change. A preview says what will happen before anything is saved: new clients, duplicates skipped (already in PlowR, or listed twice), rows that can't be imported and why (by their row in the spreadsheet), and clients who share a phone number with another (a household or an office line). Every row can be checked. The list is imported as current customers (the default) or as leads for the Pipeline, and another address for the same client can become one of their properties. After the import, map pins are looked up in the background, with progress, and addresses the map can't find are listed so they can be fixed. Undo This Import takes the import back, now or later from Recent Imports, but keeps any client worked with since.
- **Share All** in Export Data: every spreadsheet at once, to Files, Mail or AirDrop, so keeping a copy is one step instead of seven. Import Clients can bring back the Clients file's names, phones, emails, addresses, tags and notes, and the screen says what it can't.
