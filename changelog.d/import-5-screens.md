### Added
- **Import Clients** (Settings, the Clients + menu, and the empty Clients screen): bring a client list in from a spreadsheet saved as CSV, from Numbers, Excel, Google Sheets, Google Contacts or another app's export, instead of typing every client in. PlowR guesses what each column holds and shows an example from it, so a wrong guess is easy to change. A preview says what will happen before anything is saved: new clients, duplicates skipped (already in PlowR, or listed twice), rows with another client's phone under a different name (skipped, since it may be a household, an office line or the same client renamed, and the business knows which), and rows that can't be imported and why (by their row in the spreadsheet). Every row can be checked, and after the import **What Was Skipped** lists every row left out, with its row and the reason, and can be shared to keep. The list is imported as current customers (the default) or as leads for the Pipeline, and another address for the same client can become one of their properties. After the import, map pins are looked up in the background, with progress. An address the map can't find isn't tried again: the client or property is marked "Address not found on the map" in the client list and on their page until it has a pin, so the business can correct the address or set the pin by hand (a property's page now has Set Pin on the Map too). Undo This Import takes the import back, now or later from Recent Imports, but keeps any client worked with since.
- **Share All** in Export Data: every spreadsheet at once, to Files, Mail or AirDrop, so keeping a copy is one step instead of seven. Import Clients can bring back the Clients file's names, phones, emails, addresses, tags and notes, and the screen says what it can't.
