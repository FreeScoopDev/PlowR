### Added
- **Expenses summary** (Home → Expenses, at the top): for a period, **Income**, **Spent** and **Net** tiles, what was spent in each category, and the distance logged. With expenses logged, an operator wanted the answer: what this month or this season cost, how far they drove, and what's left after income. Income is money received in the period, the same figure the Season Report uses, never invoice totals. Periods are This Month, Last Month, This Year and Last Year, plus This Season and Last Season once **Business Profile → Season Starts** is set (a season runs twelve months from the 1st of that month); until then Last 12 Months, with a line pointing to Season Starts. The choice is remembered. Free for every plan. No tax estimates or mileage rates.
