### Changed
- The route screen is the first in the 1.7 design: forest green and
  copper, with Barlow for the client's name, the minutes away and Complete
  Stop. Complete Stop is one big copper button at glove height, Navigate
  and Record Services sit side by side under the stop, and a bar of
  segments shows how far through the route you are. Built for reading at a
  glance in a truck, early or late, in light or dark.
- Some labels changed with it: "Mark Stop Complete" is now Complete Stop,
  "Done — Notify Next Client" is Complete · Text Next Client, "Current
  Stop" is NOW · STOP and its number, and "Up Next" is NEXT UP. Record
  Services shows how many services are recorded beside its name.
- A stop's number in a route's list (the route screen, route details,
  building or editing a route) is forest green, green once done.

### Fixed
- The route map opens with you and your next stop both in view, the
  stop's name showing: it used to put the stop's pin on the map's top
  edge. When the stop is more than about 12 km away, the map shows where
  you are instead of miles of empty road.
- Re-center shows only after you move the map yourself (drag, pinch,
  rotate or double-tap); it used to show as soon as the screen opened, and
  the map then stopped following you. It's also bigger, easier to hit with
  gloves.
- The weather on the route screen is readable in dark mode (it was white
  text on a light grey there).
