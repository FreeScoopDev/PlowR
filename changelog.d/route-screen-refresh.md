### Changed
- The route screen is the first in the 1.7 design: forest green and
  copper, Barlow for the client's name, the minutes away and the buttons.
  Complete Stop is one big copper button at glove height, Navigate and
  Record Services sit side by side under the stop, and a bar of segments
  shows how far through the route you are. Built for reading at a glance
  in a truck, early or late, in light or dark.

### Fixed
- The route map opens with you and the stop both in view, the stop's
  name showing: it used to put the stop's pin on the map's top edge.
  Re-center shows only after you move the map yourself; it used to show
  as soon as the screen opened, and the map then stopped following you.
- The weather on the route screen is readable in dark mode (it was white
  text on a light grey there).
