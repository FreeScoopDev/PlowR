### Changed
- At the largest text sizes (Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Larger Text), the list rows now stack instead of squeezing their text beside the amounts and badges. A document's, client's and recap stop's name and address take the full width with the amount, status or services under them, a phone number or amount stays whole, and the pencil and chevron stay at the edge. Route stops list their last visit and average on separate lines, Home's Today card puts the route's stop count under its name, and Coming Up shows each date as "Mon, Nov 9" next to the name instead of in a narrow column. Text at the default and the larger non-accessibility sizes looks as before.
