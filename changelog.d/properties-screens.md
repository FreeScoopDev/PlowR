### Added
- Other Properties: a client's page lists the other places you work for them (a rental, a second lot), and Add Property adds one. Each has a name, an address put on the map as a client's is, route notes, a goal time, its default services, and an Active switch. Removing a property says what goes with it (its route stops and the visits not done yet) and keeps the work already done in Service History. Landlords and businesses with several sites were one client per address before, so their billing and history were split across made-up clients. The client's own section for their pin and measurements is now called Main Property.
- Add Stops shows a row for each of a client's places, so a client can be on a route at their own address and at a property. A new visit, and work logged by hand, can be at any of the client's places, and is priced for that place.
- A new visit's notes and time are filled in from the place chosen (the client's own address, or a property), and follow a change of place until typed over. Booked from the Schedule, picking a client used to leave them blank.

### Changed
- Every new route stop starts with its place's route notes: Edit Route and Create Route from the Schedule used to leave them empty, so a gate code set on the client never reached those stops.
- Record Services shows the stop's address (a property's, at a property) instead of the client's own. A stop's page says its time falls back to the property's goal at a property, and gives the client's usual time only at their own address.
