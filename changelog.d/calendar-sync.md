### Changed
- **Calendar sync is a switch in Settings, off until you turn it on** (Settings → Add Visits to Calendar). Turning it on asks for full calendar access, and PlowR then keeps a "PlowR" calendar in step with the schedule for visits from the last month to six months ahead, each with a reminder an hour before. Moving or editing a visit moves its event; skipping, cancelling or deleting it removes the event; completing it keeps the event, without the reminder, as a record. Older events stay as a record. PlowR follows the schedule itself, after every change and whenever the app opens or closes, so an edit on another device or a repeat is covered too. The switch is per device; the calendar, in iCloud, shows on the user's other devices. Why: PlowR used to ask for write-only access, which can't make a calendar or put an event in one, so calendar sync never added anything (seen on a simulator: "A client with write-only access cannot move event to a new created calendar"). Had it worked, it could never have found an event again to change or remove it: skip 15 visits on a snow day and 15 reminders would still go off. The 1.1.0 notes said removals synced; nothing ever removed an event.
- The PlowR calendar goes in your iCloud calendars, so it's on your other devices, or on this iPhone if you don't use iCloud calendars. It never goes in another account such as Google or Exchange, even one with a calendar named "PlowR": the privacy policy says client details stay on the device and in iCloud. The privacy policy's calendar row says what full access is used for.

### Fixed
- Delete Account & Data removes the PlowR calendar and every event PlowR added, with client names and addresses. It used to leave them. If PlowR's calendar access has been turned off in the Settings app, PlowR can't reach the calendar: Delete Account says so, and how to remove it, instead of reporting everything gone.
