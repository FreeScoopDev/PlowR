### Changed
- **Calendar sync is a switch in Settings, off until you turn it on** (Settings → Add Visits to Calendar). Turning it on asks for full calendar access, and PlowR then keeps a "PlowR" calendar in step with the schedule: visits from the last month and the next six months, each with a reminder an hour before. Moving or editing a visit moves its event, skipping, cancelling or deleting it removes the event, and completing it keeps the event without the reminder. It follows the schedule itself, after every change and whenever the app opens, so an edit on another device or a repeat is covered too. Why: PlowR used to ask for write-only access, which can't create a calendar or save an event to one, so calendar sync never added anything (seen on a simulator). Had it worked, it could never have changed or removed an event: skip 15 visits on a snow day and 15 reminders would still go off. The 1.1.0 notes said removals synced; nothing ever removed an event.
- The PlowR calendar goes in your iCloud calendars, so it's on your other devices, or on this iPhone if you don't use iCloud calendars, and never in another account such as Google or Exchange: the privacy policy says client details stay on the device and in iCloud. The privacy policy's calendar row says what full access is used for.
