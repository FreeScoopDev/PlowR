### Added
- **Log Work** on a client's page, beside Contact, Invoice, Schedule and Photos. Logging a job done off a route used to mean scrolling past the whole form to Service History and its +; that stays too.
- Home's **Quick Actions** gain **Log Work** and **New Invoice**, now six: New Client, Schedule Visit, Log Work, New Invoice, New Route and Season Report. Both ask which client first, with the same searchable list as Documents; a client added there with **New Client** goes straight on to the job or the invoice.
- After **Create Route** on the Schedule, **Open Route** takes you to the new route, and for today's visits **Start Route** starts it, with the same checks as the route's own Start. It used to end in an alert with only OK, leaving you to find the route in the Routes tab.

### Changed
- Empty pages offer a way forward: the Routes tab with no routes has a **New Route** button, and a route with no stops says "Add clients to this route." with an **Add Stops** button. It used to say "Tap Edit", but there was no Edit button on the page.
- **Select Client** with no clients yet says what the client is for, where it always talked about proposals.
