### Fixed
- **Invoices from a route no longer bill a client once per mapped zone.** Record Services shows one price for a per-square-foot service: the rate times the client's whole mapped area. Saving wrote that whole-property price onto every zone's line, so a client with two zones was billed twice and one with three zones three times, without anyone typing anything. The price is now split across the zones in proportion to their area, in whole cents, so the lines always add up to the figure the driver saw. A client with one zone, or none, was never affected.
- **Duplicating or revising a document keeps every line's amount.** Copies recomputed each line from quantity × unit price, which silently undid any amount typed over the catalog price, including edits made on the invoice's Edit screen.
- **"Group line items by service" keeps the total.** A grouped line used to take the last line's unit price, so two flat lines for the same service became one line at a single price and typed amounts were dropped. A grouped line's total is now the sum of the lines it replaces.
