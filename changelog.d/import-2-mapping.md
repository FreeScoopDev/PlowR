### Internal
- Import Clients' matching (`ClientImport`), groundwork for importing a client list from a spreadsheet. Each column's field is guessed from its header as other apps and people write it, by its words when it isn't an exact match ("Client Phone #", but never a column about a field, like Google's "Phone 1 - Label" or "Email Opt In"), and among like columns the one most filled in (Outlook's Business, Home and Mobile phones): a service address over a billing one, First + Last Name, City, State and ZIP added to an address only when it doesn't already hold them, Google Contacts' labels as tags, and a company kept in the notes beside a person's name. A preview sorts every row into new, a client PlowR already has (the same name with the same phone, or with the same address; "Street" and "St" match, an empty address matches nothing), another property of one (only when asked, and to the right client), or a row with no name, named by its row in the spreadsheet (blank rows count). The same phone at the same address is the same client under any name; at another address under another name it's a new client, said in the preview, because households and office lines share numbers. A phone's extension is ignored, but not an "x" in "Fax" or "Box". Rows are matched through an index, so thousands of rows against thousands of clients take a moment rather than the 25 seconds a pairwise check took.
