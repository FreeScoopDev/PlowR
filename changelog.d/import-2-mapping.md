### Internal
- Import Clients' matching (`ClientImport`), groundwork for importing a client list from a spreadsheet. Each column's field is guessed from its header as other apps and people write it, by its words when it isn't an exact match ("Client Phone #"): a service address over a billing one, First + Last Name, an "Address" column beside City, State and ZIP taken as the street so none of them is lost, Google Contacts' labels as tags, and a company kept in the notes beside a person's name. A preview sorts every row into new, a client PlowR already has (the same name with the same phone, or with the same address; "Street" and "St" match, an empty address matches nothing), another property of one (only when asked, and to the right client), or a row with no name. The same phone under another name is a new client, said in the preview, because households and office lines share numbers. Rows are matched through an index, so thousands of rows against thousands of clients take a moment rather than the 25 seconds a pairwise check took.
