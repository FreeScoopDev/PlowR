### Added
- Contract visits (contracts, step 4). A contract can book its own visits: its days of the week, every week or every few weeks, at each place it applies to, through its last day ("Every Tuesday", "Every 2 weeks on Tuesday and Friday"). Once it's signed, Book Visits on its page puts them on the Schedule, at 8 AM with the place's notes and goal time and the contract's services, each movable like any visit; they're ordinary repeating visits, so routes and the calendar treat them as any other. A day that already has a visit at that place is left as it is: one of its own done, skipped or cancelled (booking again never undoes that), or one booked by hand (a client already visited every Tuesday isn't booked twice); the confirmation says how many. Changed its days? Book Again replaces the visits it booked that aren't done yet. Cancelling it, moving its end earlier, or switching its visits off takes off its visits after that day. An inactive client isn't booked. Add Visit and contracts share one weekday picker. Visits are booked by a tap, not in the background: several devices booking by themselves would each book the season. Snow contracts usually leave this off. Before, a season of weekly mowing had to be set up as a separate repeating visit, with nothing tying it to the agreement.
