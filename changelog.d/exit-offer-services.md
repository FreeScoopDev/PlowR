### Changed
- The text to the next client that PlowR offers shortly after you drive away from a stop now records that stop's expected services too, and says so. Its sheet names the stop you left and every service it will record, the same ones the "Done:" button names; **Send Message** or **Skip** completes the stop and records them. Before, it completed the stop with no services and didn't say so, so a crew that drove off without tapping "Done:" still ended up with a record saying "No services", the gap the "Done:" button was meant to close. When the stop has nothing to add (nothing expected, something already recorded, already on an invoice), the sheet says it records nothing new. **Cancel** completes nothing, leaving **Complete Without Services** on the stop; Undo takes the services back off. Leaving a stop still never completes it by itself, and when the text is offered is unchanged.
