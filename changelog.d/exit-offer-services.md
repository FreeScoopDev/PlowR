### Changed
- The text to the next client that PlowR offers shortly after you drive away from a stop now records that stop's expected services too, and says so. Its sheet names the stop you left and every service it will record, the same ones the "Done:" button names; **Send Message** or **Skip** completes the stop and records them. Before, it completed the stop with no services and didn't say so, so a crew that drove off without tapping "Done:" still ended up with a record saying "No services", the gap the "Done:" button was meant to close. When the stop has nothing to add (nothing expected, something already recorded, already on an invoice), the sheet says it adds no services. If something changed while the sheet was up and fewer services were recorded than it named, PlowR says which ones weren't and where to add them. **Cancel** completes nothing, leaving **Complete Without Services** on the stop; Undo takes the services back off. Leaving a stop still never completes it by itself, and when the text is offered is unchanged. Lists of services in this copy always read in English, whatever the phone's region.
