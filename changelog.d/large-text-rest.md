### Changed
- At the largest text sizes (Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Larger Text), the rest of the screens now fit. On the route screen, "Message All" sits under "NEXT UP" and wraps at the space, each next stop keeps its navigate button inside the card, the stop's phone number stays whole, "N done" goes under "Stop N of M", and the map is shorter so the stop card starts higher. The Undo bar puts "Completed <client>" over the Undo button instead of cutting the name. Camera and Library stack on Record Services, and a service's name sits above its price and switch when building an invoice or proposal. The route's name wraps on its own page instead of ending in "Monday Nort…". The business profile and a client's name, phone, email, street address and route notes grow to several lines instead of hiding their value, and the Pro screen's close button sits above the text instead of over it. Text at the default and the larger non-accessibility sizes looks as before.
- Fixed: on a route's page, a stop skipped for today squeezed its name, address and details into a one-letter column. Its "Skipped Today" chip now sits under the row, so the details keep their width at every text size.
