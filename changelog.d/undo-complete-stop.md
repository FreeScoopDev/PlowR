### Added
- Undo after Complete Stop. A stop completed by mistake (a gloved tap, a bump, the wrong stop) used to be recorded for good, and the route moved on with no way back. For 30 seconds after a stop is completed, the route screen shows "Completed <client> · Undo"; Undo takes back exactly what completing did (the client's visit count, time and last service date, the Service Log record, which is deleted if completing made it or put back as it was if Record Services had, including its invoice link, the day's visit and the next one its series added, the stop's time and the departure being watched) and the route returns to that stop with its timer as it was. It works the same after a completion from the text-next-client sheet, Siri or Control Center while the route screen is up. Only the stop just completed can be undone, and not once another stop is completed or passed, the route ends or changes on another device, or after the last stop (the recap opens and edits a stop itself). A text already sent stays sent. Nothing is kept across a relaunch: if iOS ends PlowR inside the 30 seconds, the completion stands.
