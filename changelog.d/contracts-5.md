### Added
- Contracts, step 5 (the last of the plan):
  - **A proposal becomes a contract.** A proposal has a Contract button: a draft contract with the proposal's services (those in the catalog, matched by name; none matched, they're picked by hand), its total before tax as the season price (a contract's payments carry no tax of their own) and its notes, to check and mark signed. Once a contract is made from it, the proposal shows View Contract instead, and can't be made into a second contract or converted to an invoice: the season isn't billed twice.
  - **A contract to share.** A contract's page shares it as a PDF in the business's colours and logo, as its proposals and invoices are: the business and the client, the period and properties, the services it covers, the price and each payment's date, the visits it books, the snow trigger, the terms (long terms run on to further pages), and lines for both to sign, kept together. The file is named for the client and the contract. Signing stays by hand (Mark Signed): e-signatures would need a server.
  - **Renew.** A signed contract that wasn't cancelled can be renewed once: a new draft for the same months next season (a winter contract renews into next winter, a year's into the next year), with the same terms, at the same price or raised 3, 5 or 10%. Renewed long after it ended, it's the next season that hasn't passed. A name made from its dates is made from the new ones.
  - **Export Data** has a Contracts file: each contract, its client, where it stands, its dates, pricing and price, payments, services, visits, and when it was signed or cancelled.
