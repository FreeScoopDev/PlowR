### Added
- Delete Account & Data offers **Export My Data First**, which opens Export Data, and says what the export is: a copy to keep, as spreadsheets, that can't be loaded back into PlowR and doesn't include routes, photos or settings (photos are shared from each client's Photos page). Joe asked for a way to keep a copy before removing anything.
- Export Data has two more files: **Proposals** (quotes not yet invoices) and the **Schedule** (every scheduled visit, past and ahead).
- Settings → Data & Backup explains how sync works: through your private iCloud to every device on your Apple ID, Remove from This Device to take the data off one device (which turns sync off there until you turn it back on), and Export Data for a copy.
- PlowR on a Mac (the iPad app on Apple silicon): Settings has an **On a Mac** section saying what works differently there, and the route screen says it in a line at the top. On a Mac PlowR doesn't follow your location during a route (no GPS arrival and departure times, no offer to text the next client on leaving a stop, no arrival estimates from where the Mac is), can't text, has no camera, and shows no Live Activity or Control Center control; everything else works the same and syncs through iCloud.

### Changed
- On a device that can't text (an iPad without Messages, a Mac), the route no longer offers to text the next client: the stop button says Mark Stop Complete and moves on, as for a client who'd rather not be texted, instead of opening a prompt that could only be skipped.
