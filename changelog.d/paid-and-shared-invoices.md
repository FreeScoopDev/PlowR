### Changed
- **Marking an invoice paid ends "Awaiting Response"**: a payment is the client's response.
- **Sharing a draft invoice to someone marks it sent**, with its due date, as Mark Sent does. That includes a new invoice shared from its preview and then saved, as long as it's saved as the invoice that was shared: not one rebuilt after going back to edit it, and not one whose number had to change because another invoice took it meanwhile (share that one again). It used to stay a draft, with no due date running, though the client had it.
