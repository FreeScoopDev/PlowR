### Changed
- **Marking an invoice paid ends "Awaiting Response"** when it's what the client was awaiting: a payment is their response. A document sent after it keeps them awaiting.
- **Sharing a draft invoice to someone marks it sent**, with its due date, as Mark Sent does, including one shared from the new invoice's preview before it's saved. It used to stay a draft, with no due date running, though the client had it.
