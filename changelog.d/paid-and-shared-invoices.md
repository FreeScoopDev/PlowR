### Changed
- **Marking an invoice paid ends "Awaiting Response"**: a payment is the client's response.
- **Sharing a draft invoice to someone marks it sent**, with its due date, as Mark Sent does. That includes a new invoice shared from its preview and saved from that same preview. Going back to the form makes a new copy, which is saved as a draft; so is an invoice whose number another invoice took meanwhile, as it's saved with the next number. Share or Mark Sent either one again. It used to stay a draft, with no due date running, though the client had it.
