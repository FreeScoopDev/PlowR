### Changed
- A route stop's minutes now run from the GPS arrival at the stop to its completion when PlowR caught the arrival, instead of from when the stop became the current stop. The old count included the drive there, which inflated each client's total service time and the route's averages. With no arrival caught (location off or approximate, already inside the area when it was placed, a gap, a Mac) nothing is guessed: the minutes are counted as before. The Service Report line says which it is ("min, GPS arrival to completion" or "min, including travel"), and the report's note, the job page footer and the help say both cases in plain words. The route recap's On Route time is now the run's elapsed time, from its start to the last completion, because the stops' minutes added up no longer include the driving. Minutes already saved are not changed, and no price reads them. No CloudKit schema change: no field was added, the same stored numbers are written.
