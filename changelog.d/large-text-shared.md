### Changed
- At the largest text sizes (Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Larger Text), the shared pieces now give way instead of cutting words off. A row of number tiles (Home's Money, the Clients, Documents and route summaries) stacks, one under another, so "Outstanding" and "Services" read in full. The client page's four actions sit two by two. Home's Quick Actions are one to a row. Icons grow with the text and, beside text, move above it, so Home's iCloud note, Needs You and Today rows, the Pro paywall, and the setup, export and role screens aren't left with a sliver of width. Text at the default and the larger non-accessibility sizes looks as before.
