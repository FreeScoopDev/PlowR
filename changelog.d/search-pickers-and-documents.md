### Added
- Search wherever you pick a client: a new proposal or invoice's **Select Client**, **Add Visit**'s client and a route's **Add Stops**. With dozens of clients, finding one meant scrolling a long list; now every picker searches the same way as the Clients list.
- **Documents** can be searched (client, invoice number or notes) and sorted by Newest, Due Date, Amount or Client from the filter button. The sort is remembered; Newest is the order the list always had.

### Changed
- Client search, in the Clients list and every picker, ignores accents and finds a client when every word typed is in their name, address, phone or tags ("park alder" finds Ellen Park on Alder Ct), and a phone number typed without dashes still finds it.
- Documents with nothing under a filter now say what the filter shows (no invoices, no drafts, nothing overdue) and offer New Invoice or New Proposal to match, where it always used to talk about proposals.
- The Documents filter button is read by VoiceOver as "Filter and Sort".
