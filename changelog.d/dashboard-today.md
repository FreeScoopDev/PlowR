### Changed
- The Dashboard puts today first. At the top: the date, your business
  (tap it for its profile) and the weather. Then today's route, the one
  you ran most recently, with a big Start Route button that opens it ready
  to go, and today's visits. Then Needs You: invoices overdue, contract
  payments due (Make Invoices right there), invoices ready to send, quotes
  to follow up, and records to check, each opening where it's dealt with,
  or "All caught up". Then money owed and money in this month, leads and
  quotes, what's coming up, and quick actions. The business card, the
  separate Routes and Finances cards and the coloured weather strip are
  gone; what they showed is in the sections above.
- With nothing on today, the Dashboard offers New Route and Schedule Visit
  instead of an empty card.
