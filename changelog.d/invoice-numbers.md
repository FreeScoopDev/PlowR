### Fixed
- **Deleting an invoice no longer makes the next one repeat a live invoice's number.** The next number was the count of invoices plus one, worked out separately on four screens: delete INV-0003 of five and the next invoice was a second INV-0005. The next number is now one past the highest already used, from one place (`InvoiceNumbering`). A revision holds its original's number, so revising INV-0007 and then deleting the original doesn't hand INV-0007 out again. The builder numbers an invoice when it's built, so the preview can show it, and takes the next free number at save if that one has been taken meanwhile. Two devices creating invoices before they sync can still pick the same number.
- **Revision numbers work the same way.** Deleting a revision no longer repeats its number, and revising a revision gives INV-0042-R2 rather than INV-0042-R1-R1. The "Revise Paid Invoice" message now names the number the copy will actually get; it always said -R1.
