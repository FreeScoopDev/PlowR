### Internal
- No APIs deprecated in iOS 26 are left, so a Release build has no warnings at all. The client side's business search and property scan look addresses up through `AddressPin.coordinate(of:)` (`MKGeocodingRequest`), as the rest of the app already did, instead of `CLGeocoder`; business results and address suggestions read a map item's `location` and `address` instead of its `placemark`; route ordering's drive-time requests build map items from a location; and the Schedule's calendar takes the screen width from its own window instead of `UIScreen.main`. They still worked, but Apple will remove them, and a clean build makes a new warning stand out.
