Common questions

Where is my data kept?

On your devices and in your own iCloud account, synced between your iPhone, iPad and Mac signed in to the same Apple ID. PlowR has no servers, so we can't see your data, and we can't recover it for you. See the Privacy Policy for details.

My data isn't showing on my other device.

Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple ID, that iCloud is on for PlowR (Settings › your name › iCloud), and that both have a connection. The first sync of a large business can take a few minutes. PlowR's Settings › Data & Backup shows whether iCloud is working.

How do I bring my clients in from another app or a spreadsheet?

Save the list as a CSV file (Numbers, Excel and Google Sheets can all do this), then use Settings › Import Clients. PlowR shows what it will add before it saves anything, and an import can be undone.

How do I keep a copy of my data?

Settings › Export Data makes spreadsheets of your clients, invoices, proposals, payments, contracts, schedule and service history. Share All sends them together, to Files, Mail or AirDrop.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › Delete Account & Data. Remove from This Device clears this device and leaves your iCloud copy; Delete Everything deletes your data from this device and from iCloud, on every device. Export a copy first if you might want it later.

Does PlowR work on a Mac?

Yes, on Macs with Apple silicon. Some things need a phone: texting clients, GPS arrival times at stops and the Lock Screen route. Settings › On a Mac lists the differences.

Does PlowR take payments?

No. PlowR makes invoices and tracks what's been paid, but payments happen however you and your clients agree. Your payment methods (a payment link, checks, cash) can be printed on invoices.
