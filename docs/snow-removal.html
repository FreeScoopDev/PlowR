PlowR

A snow plow and snow removal app for small businesses

If you plow driveways and lots, shovel walks and spread salt, PlowR keeps the season on your iPhone: who's on contract, what the storm will do to them, the route to clear them, and a record of every push.

Download PlowR on the App Store

Before the storm

  • Snow triggers on contracts. Each snow contract can start at every snowfall or at a depth you set, such as 2 inches PRO.
  • A storm card on Home. When the forecast for today or the next two days reaches a trigger, the card says roughly how much snow is coming and which clients' triggers it reaches.
  • An alert the evening before a storm of an inch or more, at 6 PM, if you allow notifications.
  • Tell your clients. Text the clients whose trigger it reaches from the storm card PRO, or message everyone on a route.

The forecast is a weather model's estimate for your area over the whole day, not a measurement at each property. PlowR says so on the card, and sends nothing by itself.

During the storm

  • Your route, stop by stop. Stops in your order or ordered by driving distance, the current stop on the Lock Screen as a Live Activity, and Complete Stop from Control Center.
  • What you did at each stop. Snow plowing, salting, walkway shoveling, roof snow removal or your own services, with notes and before and after photos.
  • Arrival and departure from GPS. With location set to Always, PlowR notes when you got to each property and when you left. When it didn't see one, it records nothing rather than guessing.
  • Below trigger. Not enough fell at a place? Mark it below trigger at the stop and move on PRO; no visit is recorded, and the day is kept for the report.

After the storm

  • A Service Report for any property PRO: every visit in a season or the dates you choose, with times, GPS arrival and departure, services, photos, the estimated weather for each day, and the days below trigger. For a client who questions a bill, or an insurer asking about a slip-and-fall claim. It's your own record, not a certified one. See a sample (PDF).
  • Contracts and invoices. A season price, a price per visit or a monthly amount, with installments; invoices as PDFs with your logo, and payments tracked as they come in.

When the snow melts

Lawn care, cleanup and landscaping use the same clients, routes and schedule, with repeating visits for weekly mowing. One app, all year.
