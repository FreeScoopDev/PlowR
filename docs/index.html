Service Reports · PlowR Pro

Show what you did at each visit

Put together a PDF for one property and the dates you choose. A Service Report can include recorded times, completed services, notes, photos and estimated weather.

GPS arrival and departure times appear when available with the required location setting. The report identifies where the information came from and lists days a contract’s snow trigger was below threshold when recorded.
