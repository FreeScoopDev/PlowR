PlowR

Run your snow and lawn routes, and keep proof of every visit.

PlowR is an iPhone and iPad app for owner-operators and small crews who plow, shovel, mow and look after properties: work your route stop by stop, hand over a Service Report when someone asks what was done, and send the invoice.

The Service Report: what you did, visit by visit

Proof of service, as a PDF PRO

When a client disputes a bill, or an insurer asks about a slip on a walkway you cleared, make a Service Report for that property and any period: a contract's season, the last 30 days, this year, or dates you choose. For each visit it shows:

  • when the stop started and was completed, for visits run on a route;
  • arrival and departure as the phone's GPS saw them, with location set to Always;
  • the services, notes, and before and after photos, with the time each was taken where PlowR knows it;
  • the estimated weather for the visit's day.

It also lists the days a contract's snow trigger wasn't reached. It says only what PlowR recorded, and where each record came from. It's your own business record, not a certified one.

Storm triggers for snow contracts

Give each snow contract a trigger: every snowfall, or a depth such as 2 inches. When the forecast for today or the next two days reaches it, Home shows a storm card with the clients whose trigger it reaches, ready for you to text them in one go PRO and start the route. Before a storm of an inch or more, an alert at 6 PM the evening before says how much is forecast and how many triggers it reaches.

Less fell at a place than forecast? Mark it below trigger, from the storm card or right at the stop PRO: the stop starts skipped on its route, and the Service Report lists the day.

The storm card is a weather model's forecast for your area, not a measurement at each property, and it says so. Nothing is sent without you.

Everything else a property-service business runs on

  • RoutesPut stops in order, or let PlowR order them by driving distance. Work through them stop by stop, with a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and Complete Stop in Control Center.
