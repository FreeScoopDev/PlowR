PlowR

Routes, clients, scheduling and invoices for snow removal, lawn care and landscaping businesses.

PlowR runs a property-service business from your iPhone, iPad or Mac: who your clients are, where you're going today, what was done, and what's owed. It's built for small crews and owner-operators.

PlowR is in testing now and will be on the App Store soon.

What it does

  • Clients and propertiesContact details, notes, tags and every place you work for each client. Import your list from a spreadsheet.
  • RoutesBuild routes, put stops in order, and work through them stop by stop, with a Live Activity on the Lock Screen.
  • ScheduleOne-time and repeating visits, with an optional calendar of your own.
  • Proposals and invoicesPDFs with your logo, sent from your phone, with payments tracked as they come in.
  • ContractsSeasonal and per-visit agreements, with installments and the visits they cover.
  • Service recordsA log of every job with times and photos, and a Service Report of what you recorded, to share when a client or insurer asks.
  • WeatherForecasts for your stops, and a storm card when snow is on the way.
  • Your data, your iCloudEverything stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. There are no PlowR servers.
