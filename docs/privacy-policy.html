Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PlowR ("the App", "We", "Us") handles your information when you use our iOS application. PlowR is built for property-service operators — snow removal, lawn care, landscaping, and related trades — to manage clients, plan routes, and generate proposals and invoices. People looking for a service can also use it to find a local business and send them a request.

Summary

PlowR is a local-first app. We do not operate servers, do not sell your data, and do not run ads, analytics or tracking. Your business and client data is kept on your device and in your own private iCloud account; the developer has no access to it, and it is never sent to us. To work, PlowR sends some data to the services listed under Third-Party Services: addresses and locations to Apple Maps for maps, directions and arrival estimates, and coordinates to two free weather and elevation services. Like any internet request, these requests carry your device's IP address.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, or usage tracking of any kind. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may give us crash reports and overall usage figures; they don't contain your PlowR data.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking frameworks, and nothing in PlowR tracks you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not process payments. PlowR records payments you tell it you've received, and an invoice PDF can show your own payment details (a payment handle or QR code) for your client to pay you directly; no money moves through PlowR.
  • We do not operate any servers, and none of your data is sent to us.
  • We do not sell your data, or share it with anyone for advertising or any purpose of theirs. It goes to the services below only so they can answer PlowR's request.

Third-Party Services

PlowR uses the following services. None receives your account information, and none is told who you are; like any internet request, each sees your device's IP address, and may keep it in its own logs under its own policy.

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, iCloud sync (CloudKit), Apple Maps (address suggestions and lookups, directions and arrival estimates, Look Around, business search) and Siri, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) — weather conditions, the 7-day forecast, and past weather for a Service Report, based on coordinates rounded to about 1 km. Free and keyless.
  • Open Topo Data (opentopodata.org) — terrain elevation for the areas you measure on a property. Free and keyless.
  • Google Maps or Waze — only if you choose one for directions; the stop's destination is handed to that app.

Account Data and Deletion

Your data is stored on your device and in your private iCloud account, so you're in control of it. To delete it:

  • In-app (business): Go to Settings → Delete Account & Data and choose one:
    • Remove from This Device removes PlowR's data from this device only: your iCloud and your other devices keep it. It signs you out, removes PlowR's settings, the route in progress, notifications and shared files, turns iCloud sync off for PlowR on this device, and removes PlowR's database when PlowR next opens. Anything the device hasn't sent to iCloud yet is lost, so be online first. The “PlowR” calendar is left, since it's part of your calendars. Turn sync back on in Settings → Data & Backup to bring your data back.
    • Delete Everything removes all clients, properties, routes, visits, service history, proposals, invoices, payments, contracts, photos, service requests and settings from your device, ends any route in progress, removes the “PlowR” calendar's events, and signs you out. When iCloud sync is working on this device, it also removes your data from your private iCloud, and so from your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID. Do it with your device online: the deletion reaches iCloud while PlowR is open and connected. While sync is off on a device, after Remove from This Device, Delete Everything isn't offered there, since it couldn't reach iCloud: turn sync back on and reopen PlowR first, or delete from another device.
  • In-app (people looking for a service): On the home screen, open the menu and choose Delete My Data. This removes your saved requests and contact details. Requests you've already sent stay with the businesses you sent them to.
  • Uninstall: Deleting the app removes its data from your device. It doesn't remove the “PlowR” calendar (delete it in the Calendar app, or turn off Add Visits to Calendar first), and it doesn't remove your data from iCloud: use Delete Account & Data → Delete Everything for that before deleting the app. iOS may also keep PlowR's sign-in identifier in your Keychain.

Children's Privacy
