Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PlowR ("the App", "We", "Us") handles your information when you use our iOS application. PlowR is built for property-service operators — snow removal, lawn care, landscaping, and related trades — to manage clients, plan routes, and generate proposals and invoices.

Summary

PlowR is a local-first app. We do not operate servers, do not sell your data, and do not run ads or analytics. All your business and client data stays on your device and in your own private iCloud account — the developer has no access to it. A small amount of anonymous location data is sent to free public APIs solely to retrieve weather and elevation information.

What We Access and Why

Data Purpose Stored or shared?
Apple Sign In (anonymized user ID) Authenticate your operator account Stored in your device's Keychain only. Apple provides an anonymized identifier — we never receive your Apple ID email or password.
Business & client data (names, phones, addresses, routes, service history, proposals, invoices, photos) Core app functionality Stored on your device and synced to your own private iCloud account via Apple CloudKit. The developer cannot access this data. Never transmitted to any other server.
Location — When In Use Display your position on the route map and calculate stop ETAs Used on-device only during an active route. Not stored persistently after the session ends.
Location — Background (Always) Detect when you leave a job site (geofencing) to prompt client notification Handled on-device by iOS region monitoring. Your location is not transmitted to any server for geofencing.
Approximate location (~1 km precision) Retrieve local weather for the Dashboard and next-day weather alerts Sent to Open-Meteo — a free, keyless public weather API. Coordinates are rounded before transmission. No user identity is included.
Property coordinates Retrieve terrain elevation during property scans Sent to Open Topo Data — a free, keyless public API. No user identity is included.
Camera Capture before/after photos at job sites; scan business cards to auto-fill a new client (on-device OCR) Photos are stored on your device and synced to your private iCloud. Business card scanning is performed entirely on-device using Apple's Vision framework — no image is transmitted externally.
Contacts (optional) Pre-fill client name, phone, and address when adding a new client Uses Apple's system contact picker — the app receives only the single contact you choose. No contacts data is transmitted externally.
Calendar Add scheduled visits to Calendar.app with a 1-hour reminder Written to your local Calendar using Apple EventKit. No calendar data is read or transmitted beyond your device.
Notifications Weather alerts the evening before severe forecast days; overdue invoice reminders Local notifications only — no server or push token is involved.

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time in iOS Settings → PlowR.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, or usage tracking of any kind
  • We do not use advertising or tracking frameworks
  • We do not process payments — PlowR generates invoice PDFs for your own use only
  • We do not operate any servers that receive your business or client data
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for any purpose

Third-Party Services

PlowR uses the following external services. All are free, keyless public APIs that receive no account information or personal identifiers:

  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) — weather conditions and 7-day forecast based on rounded coordinates.
  • Open Topo Data (opentopodata.org) — terrain elevation for property-grade calculations.

Authentication and iCloud sync use Apple's own infrastructure and are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Account Data and Deletion

Because all data is stored in your private iCloud account, you are in full control of it at all times. To delete your account and all associated data:

  • In-app: Go to Settings → Delete Account & Data. This removes all clients, routes, proposals, and settings from your device and signs you out.
  • iCloud data: After signing out, go to iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → PlowR to remove the iCloud copy.
  • Uninstall: Deleting the app also removes all local data from your device.

Children's Privacy

PlowR is designed for business use by adults. The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.
